How it works

  1. Add both teams

    Search for players and add your roster on the left and the other team on the right. With an imported league, both rosters are already there.

  2. Mark players

    Lock players you will not trade, mark players you would give only for a good price, and mark players who must be in the trade.

  3. Pick a strategy and search

    Choose Best for me, Improve categories, Sell high, Buy low, Consolidate or Depth, set how fair the trade must be, and get five ideas at a time.

Frequently asked questions

How does the trade finder decide a trade is fair?

It compares rest of season value on both sides, including the roster spot a team must open or can fill. It also checks that the other team does not lose too many categories, ignoring categories they punt.

Do I need to import my league?

No. You can add both teams by hand and use the default 9-cat format, or change the format. Importing an ESPN, Sleeper or Fantrax league adds real rosters, free agents and standings.

What does Lock, Only for a good price and Trade mean?

Lock keeps a player out of every trade. Only for a good price lets a player be traded only when the gain is clearly bigger. Trade means the player must be part of every suggestion.

Does it work for points leagues?

Yes. In points leagues it ranks trades by fantasy points per game and makes sure the other team does not lose too many points.

Can I see the trade in more detail?

Yes. Open any suggestion in the trade analyzer to see category totals, weekly games and the full roster before and after the trade.
