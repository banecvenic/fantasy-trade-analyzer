How it works

  1. Pick your league or enter your team

    With an imported league we read every roster and the standings. Without a league, add your players and we look at all NBA players.

  2. Choose what you need

    Let RosterSwing find your weak categories, or pick the categories yourself. Punted categories never count.

  3. Open the trade

    Tap a name to see the report with a possible fair trade, up to 2 for 2. Open it in the analyzer, ask for other trades with that player, or save him to your wishlist.

Frequently asked questions

How do you choose targets?

Mostly by how much a player improves the categories you need for the rest of the season. Then we check whether his manager might trade him: does he have a surplus in those categories, does he need what you have, is he short on IR spots or near the bottom. Form, role, value versus price and playoff games add a little.

What does an unreachable player mean?

A player who is one of the two most valuable players on his team, or one of the top stars in the league. Managers rarely trade those, so we rank them lower even when they would help you a lot.

What is the wishlist?

Tap the heart on a card to save a player. The wishlist sits at the bottom of the page, shows who owns him now and his status, and stays saved in this browser for each league.
