Frequently asked questions

How do you choose the suggestions?

We value every free agent for your team with a blend of the season, the last 15 and 7 days and the projection (the projection counts less as the season goes on), weighted by the categories your team needs and without your punted categories. A player is suggested only when he is clearly better than the player you would drop. Rising minutes, an injured teammate and growing ownership move a player up a little.

Why only imported leagues?

To know who is really a free agent we need every roster in your league. Imported leagues give us all rosters and player statuses, and manual leagues usually have only some teams.

What is an IR stash?

An injured free agent who is valuable when healthy. If you have a free IR spot and your league allows his status on IR, you can add him now and keep him on IR until he returns.

Do you see waiver claims and transaction limits?

Where the platform shares it. ESPN gives the waiver list and the transaction limit, Sleeper gives recent drops, and Fantrax gives neither, so there every player is shown as a free agent.
