Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

RosterSwing is a free set of fantasy basketball tools. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no server of its own that receives your data.

What is stored and where

Everything you enter is saved only in your browser (local storage) on the device you use:

  • leagues and league settings (name, platform, roster size, standings you type in),
  • your roster and the opponent rosters you add,
  • open trades, trade history and saved trade ideas,
  • your profile name, if you enter one, and your language choice,
  • a temporary copy of player stats, so the page loads faster.

We do not receive, see or keep any of this. If you clear your browser data for this site, it is deleted. You can make a backup and delete everything from the settings in the app.

What is loaded from other services

To show stats, your browser connects directly to these services. Like any website, they can see your IP address and basic browser information:

  • ESPN fantasy data: player stats, projections, team schedules and player news,
  • ESPN image server: player photos,
  • Google Fonts: the Inter font,
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.

RosterSwing does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Shared links and AI text

A shared trade link contains only player IDs, the stats period and the language. Your rosters and history are not in the link.

"Copy for AI analysis" puts the text on your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste it yourself.
