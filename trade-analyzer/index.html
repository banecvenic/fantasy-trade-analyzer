How it works

  1. Add the players you give

    Type a name in You give. One player or a whole package.

  2. Add the players you get

    Fill in You get. The fantasy basketball trade calculator adds up both sides per game.

  3. Read the result

    See which categories get stronger or weaker, the score across all 9 categories, and a short report on minutes, trends and injuries.

What you get

  • Category by category comparison

    Points, rebounds, assists, threes, steals, blocks, FG%, FT% and turnovers, with the difference for each one.

  • Weighted FG% and FT%

    Percentages come from makes and attempts, so a high percentage on a few shots does not count as much as volume.

  • Minutes and trends

    Switch between season, last 7, 15 and 30 days, and projections. See who is playing more or less lately.

  • Injuries and news

    Injury status, missed games and the latest player news in the trade report.

  • Impact on your team

    Add your league and roster to see your team before and after the trade, with roster size and IR spots from your league.

  • Text for AI analysis

    Copy a ready summary of the trade with stats, news and your roster, and paste it into the AI assistant you use.
