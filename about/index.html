About RosterSwing

RosterSwing makes free fantasy basketball tools for people who play in category leagues and want to check a move with numbers before they make it.

What is available

  • Trade analyzer: compare both sides of a trade category by category, with weighted FG% and FT%, minutes, trends, injuries and news.
  • Your leagues: save your leagues and rosters in the browser and see your team before and after a trade.

Data

Stats, projections and player news come from publicly available ESPN fantasy data. RosterSwing is not affiliated with ESPN, Yahoo or the NBA.

Contact
