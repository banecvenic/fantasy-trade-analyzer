How to import your fantasy basketball league

RosterSwing reads public leagues only. We never ask for your password, login or cookies. Pick your platform below.

  1. ESPN
  2. Yahoo
  3. Fantrax
  4. Sleeper

Add your league

ESPN

ESPN leagues are private by default. Only the League Manager (LM) can make a league public, and only on the ESPN website, not in the app.

  1. Open your league on fantasy.espn.com in a browser.
  2. Go to League > Settings > Basic.
  3. Find Make league viewable to public and set it to Yes, then save.
  4. In RosterSwing, click Add your league > ESPN public league and paste the league ID or the league link.

A public league can be viewed, not joined. Nobody can join your league or change anything because of this setting, and the LM can switch it back at any time.

Where to find the league ID

Open the league on the ESPN website and look at the address bar. The number after leagueId= is the league ID, for example leagueId=123456. You can also paste the whole link, RosterSwing finds the ID for you.

Not the LM?

Send this message to your League Manager:

Hey, could you set our ESPN league to 'viewable to public'? It's under League > Settings > Basic on the website. It doesn't let anyone join, it just lets tools like RosterSwing read rosters so I can analyze trades.

Yahoo

Automatic Yahoo import is coming soon. Until then, use Paste roster: it takes about a minute per team.

When the Yahoo import arrives, the league will need to be publicly viewable. The commissioner turns this on with the Make League Publicly Viewable option in the league settings. This is not the same as Publish League, which is a different setting.

Fantrax

In RosterSwing choose Fantrax public league and enter the league ID or link. We import teams, rosters, scoring and standings. To make the league public, the commissioner goes to Commissioner > League Setup > Misc and turns on Allow public to view league.

The league ID is the part of the league address after /league/, for example fantrax.com/fantasy/league/abc123xyz/home.

Sleeper

In RosterSwing choose Sleeper league. We import teams, rosters, standings and the points scoring. Sleeper leagues need no settings change. The league ID is the long number in the league address, for example sleeper.com/leagues/1180226123456789012, or you can enter your Sleeper username.

Paste roster (CBS, Yahoo and other platforms)

Works with any platform that shows a roster page. You paste one team at a time.

  1. Open the roster page of one team on your platform (Yahoo, CBS, Fantrax or any other).
  2. Select the whole page with Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on a Mac) and copy it with Ctrl+C (Cmd+C).
  3. In RosterSwing, click Add your league > Paste roster, paste the text and click Find players.
  4. Check the players. Pick the right player where a name fits more than one, and type the names we could not read.
  5. Click Save and add another team to paste the next team.

Stats, positions and injury statuses in the pasted text are ignored. Only player names are used. Pasted leagues stay editable, and you can paste any team again when its roster changes.

FAQ

Is my data safe?

Yes. RosterSwing never asks for your password, login or cookies. We read only what a public league shows to everyone, and your leagues are saved in your own browser. We do not store real names of league members.

Why only public leagues?

Reading a private league needs your login cookies. We do not want to ask for them or keep them, so for now we import public leagues only.

Will private leagues be supported?

Private leagues are coming. Until then, the LM can make the league public, or you can use Paste roster.

Can strangers join my league if it is public?

No. Public means viewable, not joinable. Nobody can join or change anything, and the LM can switch the setting back at any time.
